# Analyst Perspective

This is a personal principle that guides the way I approach security
analysis and Cybersecurity in general.<br>
Cybersecurity investigations are not only about identifying malicious
artifacts or obtaining detections from security tools. Behind every
indicator there may be a person, a system, or an organization affected
by the threat.<br>
**Never lose sight of the human impact behind the indicators**. Every suspicious email represents a potential victim, and every IOC may represent a real security risk.

Tools provide evidence, but they do not replace human judgment, critical thinking, or the responsibility to understand the potential impact of an incident.<br>

The goal is not only to identify malicious artifacts, but also to understand the threat, assess the risk, and help protect the people and systems behind them.
